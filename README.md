## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 🔎 [Drishyam — Evidence Intelligence for Criminal Investigation](https://github.com/tan-ishk007/Drishyam)

**Smart India Hackathon 2026 · SIH26189 · Blockchain & Cybersecurity**

A case-workspace prototype designed to connect evidence across documents while keeping findings traceable to their source.

- Ingests evidence files such as PDFs, images, CSV/XLSX files, and text.
- Builds entity networks, timelines, transaction views, and explainable alerts from extracted information.
- Preserves evidence provenance and adds integrity mechanisms such as SHA-256 hashing, an audit chain, and Merkle proofs.
- Keeps extracted information reviewable instead of automatically treating every extraction as a confirmed fact.

**Tech:** React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Python, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, Redis, Celery, NetworkX, Docker
